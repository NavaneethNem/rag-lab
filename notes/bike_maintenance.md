# 2022 Honda Rebel 500 maintenance notes

These checks apply to the 2022 Honda Rebel 500 (CMX500). Record the odometer, service date, and actual measurements so the next inspection can show what changed. The schedule and model-specific limits come from the [Honda owner’s manual](https://cdn.powersports.honda.com/documentum/MWOM/ml.remawmom.2022_cmx500_a_a2_owner_s_manual.pdf).

## Drive chain slack

With the engine off and the transmission in neutral, park on the side stand on level ground. Place a metric ruler beside the lower chain run, halfway between the sprockets. Gently push the chain up and down. The difference between the two readings is the slack. The acceptable range is **25–35 mm**. Roll the motorcycle forward and repeat at several points on the chain. Readings of 23 mm and 33 mm warrant an inspection for binding links before adjusting. Honda sets a no-ride limit above **50 mm** and schedules chain inspection and lubrication every **600 miles**.

## Engine oil grade and intervals

Use **SAE 10W-30** motorcycle oil that meets **JASO MA** and **API SJ or higher**. Avoid bottles marked Energy Conserving or Resource Conserving. Change the oil at **600 miles**, at the **8,000-mile** service, and then every **8,000 miles** or **one year**, whichever comes first. Refill capacity is **2.5 L** after draining or **2.7 L** when replacing the filter. To verify the level with the sight glass, idle a cold engine for 3–5 minutes, shut it off, wait 2–3 minutes, and hold the motorcycle upright on level ground.

## Brake fluid and boiling points

Use fresh **DOT 4** fluid from a sealed container. The minimum dry boiling point is **230 °C (446 °F)** and the minimum wet boiling point is **155 °C (311 °F)**. The wet test represents fluid after controlled moisture exposure. Honda schedules fluid replacement every **two years**. If a level falls below the lower mark, check pad wear and have the system inspected for leaks if the pads are sound. [Federal brake fluid standard, sections S5.1.1–S5.1.2](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-V/part-571/subpart-B/section-571.116).

## Resting battery voltage

Check the 12 V AGM battery with a multimeter set to DC volts and the ignition off. Let the battery rest overnight after riding or charging, then place the probes directly on the posts. About **12.8 V** is consistent with full charge. A reading of **12.3 V** calls for charging with an AGM-compatible charger and retesting. If the starter still turns slowly after a full charge, check the terminal connections and test the battery under load. [Yuasa AGM battery guidance](https://www.yuasabatteries.com/resources/guides/spring-start-up-guidelines/).
